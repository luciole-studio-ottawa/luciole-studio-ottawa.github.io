Questions

What owners usually ask

Is it made with AI?

Partly. We use AI video tools to bring your photos to life, then we edit, caption and check every video by hand. We tell you which clips are AI-generated so you can label them where platforms ask for it.

Do you guarantee more customers?

No. Nobody honest can. We promise the videos described in your package, delivered on time and made to look like your place.

Who owns the videos?

You do, once they are paid for. Use them anywhere, for as long as you like. Your photos and logo always stay yours.

What do you need from me?

3 to 5 good photos of your dishes or space, your logo if you have one, and 5 minutes for our form. If you don't have photos, tell us and we'll find a way.

How do I pay?

By Interac e-Transfer, in advance each month, with HST added. Grand Moment takes a 50% deposit and the rest on delivery.

Do you only work with restaurants?

We started with restaurants and cafés, and we also work with small businesses that sell a product they can photograph: bakeries, beauty products, shops and more.

Want to see your place in a video?

Tell us a little about your business and we'll make your free sample ad. Prefer to talk in person? We come to you anywhere in Ottawa.
