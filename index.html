Questions

What owners usually ask

Is it made with AI?

Partly. We use AI video tools to bring your photos to life, then we edit, caption and check every video by hand. Any video you send is used as is, and we tell you when platforms ask you to label AI content.

Do you guarantee more customers?

No. Nobody honest can. We promise the videos described in your package, delivered on time and made to look like your business.

Who owns the videos?

You do, once they are paid for. Use them anywhere, for as long as you like. Your photos and logo always stay yours.

What do you need from me?

A few good photos or short videos of your products or space, your logo if you have one, and 3 minutes for our form. After that, a 2-minute form each month. If you don't have photos, tell us and we'll find a way.

How do I pay?

By Interac e-Transfer, in advance each month. Grand Moment takes a 50% deposit and the rest on delivery.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Any small business with something worth showing: restaurants and cafés, bakeries, beauty and wellness, shops, services and more.
